# App logo geometry

Apple TV and YouTube use brand silhouettes from the corresponding [Simple Icons repository](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) SVGs, retrieved 2026-10-31:

- [Apple TV](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/appletv.svg)
- [YouTube](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/youtube.svg)

The exact SVG path geometry is embedded in `index.html`. Local source copies are retained in `../downloads/appletv-source.svg` and `youtube-source.svg`. The dashboard applies no fill and a monochrome stroke as requested by the owner; this is an outline adaptation, not an assertion that the outlined variant is an official brand asset. No external images or network requests are needed by the dashboard.
