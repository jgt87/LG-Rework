# Product

## Register

product

## Users

One owner, on the couch in front of a 48" LG OLED (C2), 2 to 3 m from the screen, holding the LG Magic Remote. The TV is mainly an Xbox Series X display (HDMI 2). The job: land on a calm screen after power-on or Home, pick an input (almost always the Xbox) or a TV tool, and get back to playing in one or two presses.

## Product Purpose

A local replacement for the stock LG Home launcher: an HDMI source selector with Xbox first, a secondary row for Apple TV, YouTube and Browser, plus TV Settings, Game Optimizer and an escape to stock LG Home. The dashboard has no ads, accounts, recommendation rows or network requests; launched apps retain their normal behavior. Success: the desired input or app is a few remote presses away, the screen stays calm, and idle dimming/black cover remain in place.

## Brand Personality

Minimal, premium, quiet. Closer to the front panel of a good hi-fi or AV receiver than to a Smart TV launcher. Confident and calm; it says only what is needed and then gets out of the way.

## Anti-references

- Neon "gamer RGB" styling: glows, saturated neon, aggressive angles.
- Colourful UI in general; accents stay white and grey, colour only where it carries meaning (e.g. matching the remote's colour buttons).
- Stock Smart TV launchers (ad rows, content carousels, busy launcher bars) by implication of the product's purpose.

## Design Principles

1. One press to play: the Xbox is focused on arrival and Back always returns to it.
2. Quiet by default: nothing on screen that is not a choice or a hint for a choice.
3. Readable from the couch: 10-foot sizes and an unmistakable focus state.
4. Honest status: never claim an input switched or a signal exists without the TV saying so.
5. Kind to the panel: dim when idle, black when unattended, no static bright elements.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

10-foot legibility (text at least ~24px at 1080p for anything that matters), a high-contrast focus state visible from the couch, full operation with the D-pad/OK and with the Magic Remote pointer. Reduced motion respected via the existing media query.
